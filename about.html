About Us

Academy of Emergency Medicine (AEM)

AEM is an academy to prepare and train health care providers for Emergency Medical Service. We also provide a fundamental education in emergency management. Academy is designed to give basic concepts and planning steps to those new to the field of Emergency so that they may apply planning discipline and skills to challenges in their jobs.

Mission

To make all health care providers accurate and confident about practical knowledge and emergency skills.

Affiliation

LIHS (Life supporters Institute of Health Science)
A Mumbai based ‘not for profit’ Premier Institute for Emergency care with wide range of training programs.
