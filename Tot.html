TRAININGS OF TRAINER (TOT)

Training of Trainers (ToT) is a program that develops the training delivery skills of individuals aspiring to become trainers. It equips them with the knowledge, skills, and methodologies to effectively deliver training content to others.

KEY FEATURES

Developing Training Delivery Skills :
ToT focuses on enhancing skills related to course design, facilitation, and assessment, ensuring trainers can effectively deliver training sessions.
Practical Learning:
ToT programs often involve practical exercises, where participants can practice teaching segments and receive feedback.
Curriculum Development:
ToT may involve developing a specific curriculum and methodology to be imparted by the trainers.
Certification and Recognition:
Some ToT programs offer certifications or recognition.
Focus on Specific Areas:
ToT programs can be tailored to specific skills, or training needs. For example, a ToT program might focus on developing trainers for Emergency skills to work for Life Threatening area of Hospitals.

ELIGIBILITY : Medical Faculty , Tutors , Interested Medical Academicians .

Duration : 1 to 3 Days (As Required)
